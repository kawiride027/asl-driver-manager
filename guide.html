🔐 Logging In

When you open the app, you'll see the login screen. It's simple — pick your name and punch in your PIN.

1 Tap your name
Select David, Clint, or Dustin from the buttons at the top.
2 Enter your 4-digit PIN
Use the number pad. The dots fill in as you type.
3 Tap GO
If the PIN is correct, the app loads your dashboard. If not, it'll shake and let you try again.
Forgot your PIN? Ask your admin — PINs are set in the app config.

🔔 Alerts — Your Daily Starting Point

This is your home screen. It shows everything that needs attention in the next 7 days. Think of it as your morning checklist.

What you'll see

🔴 Red alerts = Overdue
Something should have been done already. Handle it ASAP.
🟠 Orange alerts = Due today or tomorrow
Time-sensitive. Get to these today if you can.
🟡 Yellow alerts = Due this week
On the horizon. Plan for these in the next few days.

What you can do

Tap an alert to jump straight to the action — if it's a ride-along alert, you'll land on the ride-along form with the driver pre-selected. If it's an inspection, you'll jump to the scheduler.

Tap the ✕ on the right side to dismiss an alert. You'll get a confirmation prompt first. Dismissed alerts won't come back until the next cycle.

When you see "All clear ✅" — nice work, you're caught up!

📅 Schedule — What's Coming Up

The schedule shows all upcoming tasks organized by month. The app automatically generates these based on cadence rules:

🟢 Ride-Alongs
Auto-generated every 90 days per driver. Do a ride-along, and the next one auto-schedules 90 days out.
🔵 Performance Reviews
Auto-generated every 180 days per driver (twice a year).
🟠 Coaching
Only shows up when a driver has discipline records or scored below 25/40 on a ride-along. Recurs every 30 days once triggered. Tagged as "Auto" so you know the system flagged it.
🩵 Inspections
These are manually scheduled by you using Quick Schedule (see below).

How to use it

Use the ‹ › arrows to flip between months. Use the filter buttons (All, Ride-Alongs, Inspections, etc.) to zero in on one type.

Tap any task in the list to take action on it — just like alerts.

⚡ Quick Schedule — Setting Up Future Tasks

Right below the filters, you'll see four Quick Schedule buttons. This is how you manually schedule any task for any driver.

1 Tap the button for what you want to schedule
🚗 Ride-Along, 📋 Inspection, ⭐ Review, or 💬 Coaching
2 Pick a driver from the dropdown
All active drivers are listed.
3 Tap the date field to open the calendar
Pick the day you want to do it.
4 Add notes (optional) and hit Schedule
Done! It shows up in the Schedule and will generate an alert when it's coming due.
You can also schedule from a driver's profile — open a driver from the Roster tab and tap "Schedule Ride-Along" or "Schedule Inspection" right in the modal.

🚛 Performing a Ride-Along

This is the big one. Ride-alongs are how you evaluate your drivers in the field. Here's how to do it from start to finish:

1 Go to Log → Perform Ride-Along
Or just tap a ride-along alert — it'll take you right there.
2 Select the driver and date
Driver dropdown + calendar picker. Today's date is pre-filled.
3 Rate each of the 8 categories (1-5 stars)
Tap the stars for each metric. The total score updates live at the top.

Here are the 8 things you're rating:

⭐ Professionalism
Uniform, appearance, attitude with customer
⭐ Safety
Driving habits, backing, spotting, PPE
⭐ Customer Interaction
Greeting, communication, leaves good impression
⭐ Placement Accuracy
Dumpster positioned correctly, gate clearance, surface protection
⭐ Route Efficiency
Organized stops, no unnecessary delays
⭐ Review Ask
Did they ask the customer for a Google review?
⭐ Truck Condition
Cab cleanliness, pre-trip habits
⭐ Documentation
Photos taken, notes in Docket, issues reported

Each category also has a notes field underneath the stars — use it to jot down specific observations.

Score Grades

🟢 36-40 = Excellent
Top-tier performance. Give them a high-five.
🟢 30-35 = Good
Solid work, minor things to improve.
🟡 25-29 = Needs Improvement
Some areas need coaching attention.
🔴 Below 25 = Below Standard
Triggers automatic coaching sessions on the schedule.
4 Add overall notes and photos
Scroll down for the general notes box and photo upload area.
5 Hit "Save Scorecard"
Done! The ride-along is logged and the next one auto-schedules 90 days out.
Scores below 25 will automatically trigger coaching sessions on the Schedule tab — the system flags these so you don't have to remember.

👥 Roster — Your Drivers at a Glance

The Roster tab shows all your drivers organized by team (Dumpster Drivers and Fence Crew). Each card shows the driver's name, role, and status badges.

Tap a driver to open their profile

The driver modal pops up with everything about that driver:

📊 Stat boxes at the top
Reviews This Month — tap to go to the Reviews tab. Ride-Alongs — tap to perform one. Avg Score — tap to see past scorecards. Discipline — tap to see discipline records.
⚡ Quick action buttons
"Schedule Ride-Along" and "Schedule Inspection" — these jump you to the Schedule tab with that driver pre-selected.
📋 Tabs: Overview, Scorecards, Discipline, Notes, Photos
Overview — edit review count, see recent activity. Scorecards — all past ride-along scores. Discipline — any warnings or actions. Notes — add/view private notes about the driver. Photos — all uploaded photos.

⭐ Reviews — Google Review Tracking

This tab tracks how many Google reviews each driver is getting customers to leave. It's a monthly leaderboard — a little friendly competition.

How to update a driver's review count

1 Go to Roster, tap a driver
In their profile, you'll see "Reviews This Month" with an input field.
2 Type the number and tap Save
The leaderboard updates automatically.

Tap any name on the leaderboard to see their full review history — a 12-month bar chart, their best month, total wins, and lifetime count.

Dustin can use the Sync button to pull review data from the linked Google Sheet automatically.

📝 Logging Activities

The Log tab has three sub-tabs:

General
Log a Performance Review, Coaching Session, Review Check-In, or other activity. Pick the driver, date, add notes and photos, hit Save.
Perform Ride-Along
The full ride-along scorecard (covered above). This is where the action happens.
Discipline
Record discipline actions (covered below).

⚠️ Discipline Actions

When a driver needs a formal warning or action, go to Log → Discipline.

1 Select the driver
Pick from the dropdown.
2 Choose the action type
Verbal Warning → Written Warning → Suspension → Termination. Tap the button for the appropriate level.
3 Set the date and describe the incident
Be specific — this becomes the official record.
4 Attach evidence if needed
Photos, screenshots — tap the attachment area to upload.
5 Save or Email
Save Discipline Record — saves to the system. Email Discipline Report — opens your email app with a pre-filled report you can send to HR or the driver.
Discipline records trigger automatic coaching sessions on the schedule — the system keeps you on top of follow-ups.

✅ To-Dos

Your personal task list. Simple and straightforward.

1 Type what needs to be done
In the text field at the top.
2 Set priority
🔴 High, 🟡 Medium, or 🟢 Low.
3 Tap "Add To-Do"
It shows up in the Open section. Tap a to-do to mark it complete — it moves to the Completed section.
The To-Dos tab shows a red badge with the number of open items — so you always know how many things are on your plate.

🪨 Quarterly Rocks

Big-picture goals for the quarter. These are your "rocks" — the important things that move the needle.

1 Select the quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
The app auto-selects the current quarter when you log in.
2 Add a rock
Type the goal, set priority, tap "Add Rock." The due date auto-fills as the last day of that quarter.
3 Update status as you go
Each rock can be: Not Started, On Track, At Risk, or Complete. Update them throughout the quarter.

📜 History

A chronological log of everything that's been recorded — activities, scorecards, discipline, the works. Use the driver filter at the top to see one driver's full history, or leave it on "All Drivers" for the big picture.

🧭 Navigation Tips

‹ Back Button
Appears in the top-left after you navigate between tabs. Tap it to go back to where you came from. Works like your browser's back button — it remembers your path.
Swipe the tab bar
There are 8 tabs. If you can't see them all, swipe the tab bar left/right to reveal more.
Tap alert banners
They're not just notifications — tap them to jump straight to the action. A ride-along alert takes you to the scorecard. An inspection alert opens the scheduler.
Tap schedule items
Same idea — tap a task on the schedule to take action on it right away.
Tap stat boxes in driver profiles
The four boxes at the top of a driver's profile (Reviews, Ride-Alongs, Avg Score, Discipline) are all tappable shortcuts.

👤 Who Can Do What

All three users can do everything listed above. A few things are role-specific:

David Manager
Full access to all tabs. Can schedule tasks, perform ride-alongs, log discipline, manage rocks and to-dos.
Clint Manager
Same as David — full access to everything.
Dustin Admin
Everything David and Clint can do, plus the Sync button on the Reviews tab to pull Google review data from the linked spreadsheet.
The golden rule: Start at 🔔 Alerts each morning. Tap the red ones first, then the orange, then the yellow. Everything else flows from there.
