📖 How to Use This App

🔔 Alerts — Your Daily Starting Point

This is your home screen. It shows everything due in the next 7 days. Think of it as your morning checklist.

🔴 Red = Overdue
Should have been done already. Handle ASAP.
🟠 Orange = Due today or tomorrow
Time-sensitive. Get to these today.
🟡 Yellow = Due this week
On the horizon. Plan for these soon.

Tap an alert to jump straight to the action. Tap ✕ to dismiss it (with confirmation).

📅 Schedule — What's Coming Up

All upcoming tasks by month. The app auto-generates these:

🟢 Ride-Alongs
Every 90 days per driver.
🔵 Performance Reviews
Every 180 days per driver (twice a year).
🟠 Coaching
Auto-triggered when a driver has discipline issues or scored below 25/40. Recurs every 30 days.
🔹 Inspections
Manually scheduled by you using Quick Schedule.

Use ‹ › to flip months. Use filter buttons to zero in. Tap any task to take action.

⚡ Quick Schedule

Below the filters on the Schedule tab — four buttons to manually schedule any task:

1 Tap the button
🚗 Ride-Along, 📋 Inspection, ⭐ Review, or 💬 Coaching
2 Pick a driver and date
Calendar pops up when you tap the date field.
3 Add notes and hit Schedule
It'll show up in the schedule and alert you when it's due.
💡 You can also schedule from a driver's profile in the Roster — tap "Schedule Ride-Along" or "Schedule Inspection" right in the modal.

🚛 Performing a Ride-Along

Go to Log › Perform Ride-Along, or tap a ride-along alert.

1 Select driver and date
2 Rate 8 categories (1-5 stars each)
Professionalism, Safety, Customer Interaction, Placement Accuracy, Route Efficiency, Review Ask, Truck Condition, Documentation. Each has a notes field underneath.
3 Add overall notes & photos
4 Save Scorecard
Next ride-along auto-schedules 90 days out.

Score Grades

🟢 36-40 Excellent  |  🟢 30-35 Good  |  🟡 25-29 Needs Improvement  |  🔴 Below 25 Below Standard (triggers coaching)

👥 Roster

All drivers organized by team. Tap a driver to open their profile:

📊 Stat boxes (tappable)
Reviews This Month, Ride-Alongs, Avg Score, Discipline — tap any to jump to that section.
⚡ Quick action buttons
"Schedule Ride-Along" and "Schedule Inspection" — pre-selects the driver for you.
📋 Profile tabs
Overview (edit reviews, recent activity), Scorecards (past scores), Discipline (warnings), Notes (private notes), Photos.

⭐ Reviews (Google)

Monthly leaderboard tracking Google reviews each driver earns.

Update review count
Go to Roster, tap a driver, edit "Reviews This Month" and Save.

Tap any name on the leaderboard for their 12-month chart, best month, and win history.

📝 Logging Activities

The Log tab has three sub-tabs:

General
Log a Performance Review, Coaching Session, Review Check-In, or Other. Pick driver, date, add notes & photos.
Perform Ride-Along
The full 8-metric scorecard (see above).
Discipline
Record formal actions (see below).

⚠️ Discipline Actions

1 Pick the driver
2 Choose action type
Verbal Warning › Written Warning › Suspension › Termination
3 Set date, describe incident, attach evidence
4 Save or Email
Save to the system, or Email opens a pre-filled report you can send.
⚠️ Discipline records auto-trigger coaching sessions on the schedule.

✅ To-Dos

Your personal task list. Type a task, set priority (🔴 High, 🟡 Medium, 🟢 Low), tap Add. Tap a to-do to mark it complete. The tab badge shows your open count.

🪨 Quarterly Rocks

Big-picture goals for the quarter.

1 Select the quarter (Q1-Q4)
Auto-selects the current quarter on login.
2 Add a rock with priority
Due date auto-fills as the last day of that quarter.
3 Update status as you go
Not Started › On Track › At Risk › Complete

📜 History

Chronological log of everything recorded. Use the driver filter at the top to see one driver's full history.

🧭 Navigation Tips

‹ Back button
Appears top-left after navigating. Tap to retrace your steps.
Swipe the tab bar
8 tabs — swipe left/right to see them all.
Tap alerts & schedule items
They're actionable — tapping jumps you to the right place with the driver pre-selected.
Tap stat boxes in driver profiles
All four boxes are tappable shortcuts.
Golden rule: Start at 🔔 Alerts each morning. Red first, then orange, then yellow. Everything else flows from there.
